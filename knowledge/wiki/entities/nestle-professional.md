# Nestlé Professional

Foodservice arm of Nestlé. Sells to professional kitchens (for example MAGGI Professional, DOCELLO, Nestlé Baking Chocolate, NESCAFÉ). Runs the Golden Chef's Hat Award for young chefs.

## Detail
- Commissioned the June 2026 young-chef survey ([[wiki/sources/nestle-young-chefs-survey]]).
- Among award alumni, 95% use its products and 90% expect to continue.
