# Nestlé young chefs survey

Survey of 300 young and aspiring chefs aged 18–25 in Australia and New Zealand, commissioned by Nestlé Professional, a foodservice brand. Sponsor is a supplier to professional kitchens, so the report has a commercial stake. Text extracted with pypdf from `raw/Nestle-Professional-Young-Chef-Survey-Report-2026.pdf` (25 pages).

## Key claims
- 78% use AI in some capacity. 44% only personally, 29% both work and personal, 5% only for work, 22% do not use it.
- Expected impact on their role in five years (multi-select): reduce repetitive admin 57%; menu development and recipe ideas 46%; AI-assisted training 38%; food safety and efficiency 26%; automate basic prep or cooking 25%; new digital skills needed 18%; little impact 8%.
- Staff shortages, long hours and work-life balance are the largest challenge (63%). Better pay is the top retention lever (63%).
- Automation or digital tools to reduce admin ranks 8th of 8 in what would help attract young chefs (5%).
- Mentorship is the leading source of inspiration (64%), ahead of social media (56%).

## Caveats
- Only ANZ, only chefs aged 18–25, and only 300 respondents. Not a home-cook sample.
- Sponsor has a brand interest; 95% of Golden Chef's Hat alumni use Nestlé Professional products, which is a selected group.
