# Cambom time-use data

Compilation and recalculation of BLS American Time Use Survey tables, plus government, academic, survey and market sources. Published 24 July 2026, dataset version 2026-07. Not a new respondent survey. Extracted in `raw/cambom_home_cooking_statistics_extracted.pdf`.

## Key claims
- Food prep and cleanup, 2024: 63.6 min/day among people who did it that day; 40.2 min/day across all US people 15+. Participation 63.1%.
- 2014 to 2024: all-person time rose from 35.4 to 40.2 min/day (+13.6%); participation rose from 56.3% to 63.1%.
- Women's participation 71.6%, men's 54.2% (2024).
- Food away from home was 58.5% of US food spending in 2023.
- Cambom explicitly rejects three circulating claims: "37 minutes cooking dinner", "53 minutes every night", and "90% hate cooking".

## Caveats
- Food prep and cleanup is broader than cooking.
- Participant and all-person averages use different denominators and must not be interchanged.
