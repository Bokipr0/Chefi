# Statista AI survey

Statista article, technology section, "Artificial intelligence (AI) use for home cooking in the U.S. - statistics & facts". Captured as a screenshot in `raw/statista-ai-home-cooking-us-screenshot.png`. The screenshot does not show the underlying survey, its sample size, fieldwork dates, or the publication date, so the article's figures are secondary and unverified.

## Key claims (as shown)
- Over half of U.S. consumers have used AI for some form of help with home cooking or shopping. Close to 20% use it regularly. 39.2% have never done so.
- Key insight: 24.4% occasionally use AI for food-related activities.
- Leading benefit: discovering new recipes and food, cited by slightly over half of consumers.
- 45.2% associate time savings with AI for meal planning and shopping.
- Concerns: data privacy and security (slightly under half), cost (34.1%), accuracy of recommendations (33.9%).
- Over two-thirds say they are likely or very likely to use AI recipe recommendations.
- Comfort is high for nutrition advice, recipe recommendations and meal plans. 7.9% are uncomfortable with nutrition advice.

## Caveats
- Survey source and sample not shown. Treat as secondary until the underlying survey is found.
- "Used", "occasionally" and "likely" are different measures.
