# QS Supplies survey

Article published and updated 11 November 2025. Survey of 1,000 UK and US respondents (75% US, 25% UK; 18% Gen Z, 49% millennials, 25% Gen X, 8% boomers). Fieldwork dates, recruitment and weighting not stated. Extracted in `raw/qs_supplies_ai_kitchen_confidence_extracted.pdf`.

## Key claims
- 84% US / 90% UK say they can cook a recipe from scratch unaided.
- Confidence in AI: asking ChatGPT mid-meal 54% US / 56% UK; AI weekly meal plan 44% US / 40% UK; AI auto-ordering groceries 32% US / 31% UK.
- Recipe finding: 37% use AI or search for this. The figure combines both tools.
- 20% prefer asking AI to asking a person mid-recipe.

## Caveats
- Holiday figures are plans from November 2025, not observed behaviour.
