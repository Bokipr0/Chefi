# Pro demand → home?

Professional young chefs want AI for admin and recipe ideas. Home surveys (Attest, HelloFresh, QS Supplies) report AI use and interest, but none separates the job being done. Chefi is a home device, so the transfer matters for what it should do first.

## Why it matters
If home cooks want the same mix, Chefi's early value is planning and ideas, which points at the AI service rather than at camera-based control. If they want control, the camera becomes central.

## What would answer it
- A home-cook survey that asks which job AI should do, not whether they use AI.
- Competitor products (see the market synthesis) and what they actually automate.
