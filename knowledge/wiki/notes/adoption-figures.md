# Adoption mixes use and intent

Surveys in this set report different things under the same "AI for food" label. Attest's 63.8% is past use. HelloFresh's 69% is "used or open to using". QS Supplies' confidence figures are stated intentions, and its 37% recipe figure combines AI with search.

Treat these as separate measures. Do not average them, and do not read the 69% as adoption.
