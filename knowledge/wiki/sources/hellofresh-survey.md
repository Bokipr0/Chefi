# HelloFresh survey

First annual report, fielded by Wakefield Research: online survey of 5,000 US adults 18+, 10–22 June 2025, weighted. Extracted in `raw/hellofresh_state_of_home_cooking_extracted.pdf`. Sponsor is a meal-kit company.

## Key claims
- 93% expect to cook as much or more in the next 12 months (an intention, not frequency).
- 69% have used AI to help get dinner on the table or are open to using it. The page says this is not a usage rate.
- 67% believe they could plan dinner better than AI. Among AI users, 51% think AI could do better.
- 81% use shortcuts such as meal kits or ready-to-eat meals. 25% skip some foods because they lack knife confidence.
- 85% of people planning to cook more cite the economy as the motivator.

## Caveats
- Self-reported. No full questionnaire, unweighted bases, or confidence intervals published.
