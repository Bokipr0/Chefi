# Competitors

From `Market/Possible competitors list.docx` in the Chefi project folder (copied to `raw/market-possible-competitors-list.docx`). The list is a working draft, not a researched market map.

## Listed competitors
- **StudioChef** (studiochef.ai): "the kitchen AI", soft launch on Kickstarter October 2026, about 80 followers at time of listing. Closest to Chefi's kitchen-assistant role.
- **Looki**: wearable camera agent in a necklace (Looki L1). Founded 2024, reported to have raised $20M. Competes for the camera-and-agent role.
- **Meta AI glasses**: cooking and restaurant-discovery use cases. Competes on hands-free, camera-based help without a counter device.

## What the list does not yet say
- Whether any listed product judges doneness, or only recognises ingredients or recipes.
- Whether any competitor serves the home cook specifically.
