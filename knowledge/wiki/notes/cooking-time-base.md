# Cooking time needs a base

The same BLS 2024 data gives 63.6 minutes a day for people who did food prep or cleanup that day, and 40.2 minutes a day across all people 15+. Participation is 63.1%. Both numbers are correct. They answer different questions.

Any figure for "how long people cook" needs its denominator stated before it is used. This matters for Chefi's sizing: a device for everyday use serves participants, not the whole population.
