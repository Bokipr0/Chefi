# Over half have used AI

Statista reports that over half of U.S. consumers have used AI for help with home cooking or shopping. Close to 20% use it regularly, 24.4% occasionally, and 39.2% have never used it. This is past use, so it can be compared with Attest's 63.8% past-use figure, though the two surveys differ in question and sample.
