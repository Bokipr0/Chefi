# Chefs want AI for admin

Among 300 young chefs, the most-expected AI effects are reducing admin (57%) and helping with menu and recipe ideas (46%). Only 25% expect AI to automate basic prep or cooking. The top stated benefits are for the job's routine, not for the act of cooking.

This is a professional sample. The survey does not ask whether AI should judge or control a cooking step, so the result does not settle whether a home device should act on doneness.
