# 7.9% uncomfortable with AI advice

Statista reports that 7.9% of U.S. consumers are uncomfortable with AI nutrition advice. Small, but it is the one feature in the set with reported discomfort. If Chefi adds health guidance, it should expect a minority who reject it.
