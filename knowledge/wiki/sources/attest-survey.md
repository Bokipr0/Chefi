# Attest survey

Article by Stephanie Rand, updated 4 November 2026, from Attest's proprietary US and UK survey. Extracted in `raw/attest_ai_in_the_kitchen_extracted.pdf`.

## Key claims
- 63.8% report using AI-powered food tools (regular, occasional, or once or twice). UK 66.8%, US 60.8%. Sample n=1,993 (UK 998, US 995).
- Use falls with age; 74% of ages 18–24 report use.
- Comfort with AI recipe recommendations: 71.2% US, 75.9% UK.
- Top stated concern is data privacy and security (47.7% US, 45.8% UK). Lack of human touch is 37.8% US, 40.4% UK.
- Future interest: 69.3% would try recipes from available ingredients; 58.9% smart grocery suggestions; 58.5% personalised meal planning.

## Caveats (from the extraction)
- Fieldwork dates, weighting, and full questionnaire are not stated.
- "Used", "comfortable" and "likely to try" are different measures and should not be pooled.
